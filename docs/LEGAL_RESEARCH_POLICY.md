# Legal Research and Source Policy

- Prefer India Code and relevant Punjab/Haryana government portals for statutory texts and rules.
- Prefer official Supreme Court/High Court repositories for judgments where accessible; secondary repositories may be used for discovery.
- Each seed summary is a research lead, not a verified legal opinion.
- For expanded case entries, capture court, date, citation, paragraph references, source URL, state applicability, later treatment, last-checked date and reviewer.
- Never infer a statutory section number from an offence label without checking the current official text.
- For conduct/proceedings around 1 July 2024, review BNS, BNSS, BSA and corresponding legacy codes alongside commencement, repeal, savings and transitional provisions.
- State-specific amendments and rules must be checked separately for Punjab and Haryana.
\n- The Bare Acts reader links to official sources. Locally cached text must be periodically refreshed and checked against the official publication. A cached web copy is a convenience, not proof of current consolidation.\n
